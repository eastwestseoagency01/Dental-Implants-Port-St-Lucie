What Makes a Dental Implants Periodontist Different

Gum and bone health decide whether an implant lasts. That is why this specialty focus matters.

A periodontist is trained in the tissues that support your teeth, including the gums and the jawbone. Because dental implants depend entirely on these tissues, a dental implants periodontist approach brings valuable insight to implant surgery, bone health and long term maintenance.

This matters most in demanding situations. Patients with gum disease history, thin bone, previous implant problems or multiple missing teeth often need more careful planning. When we evaluate complex dental implant cases, we look at the whole picture, from the health of your gums to how your bite will function once treatment is complete.

Our approach is simple. We protect healthy tissue, plan precisely and choose the treatment that gives you the best chance of long lasting dental implants.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about dental implants in Port St. Lucie.

What is a dental implants periodontist?

A dental implants periodontist is a dental professional focused on the gums, the supporting bone and the surgical placement of implants. This focus is valuable because healthy gum and bone tissue is the foundation of a long lasting implant.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth can be considered. Candidacy depends on your general health, gum health and jawbone. A consultation with imaging is the best way to confirm your options.

How long do dental implants last?

With good home care and regular checkups, titanium implants are designed to last for many years and often decades.

Can I get same day dental implants?

Some patients qualify for same day dental implants and one day smile restoration. Your bone quality, gum health and treatment plan decide whether this is the right path.

Can a failed dental implant be fixed?

In many cases yes. Dental implant rescue and dental implant revision can address infection, bone loss or poor placement, depending on what is causing the problem.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing a missing tooth. A titanium post is placed in the jaw to act as a new root, then topped with a custom crown that matches your natural teeth. Because the implant stands on its own, neighboring teeth stay untouched, and your bite stays strong.

As a dental implants periodontist focused practice, we carefully evaluate your gums and bone before implant surgery so your tooth implant has the best foundation. This planning supports comfortable healing and long lasting dental implants that look and feel natural.

Whether you need one tooth or several, our implant procedure is tailored to your needs, your comfort and your timeline.

Full Arch and All on 4 Dental Implants

If you are missing most or all of the teeth in an arch, full arch dental implants can bring back a complete, fixed smile. All on 4 dental implants use four strategically placed implants to support a full set of teeth, often reducing the need for extensive bone grafting.

This permanent teeth implant treatment helps you chew with confidence and speak clearly, without the movement of traditional dentures. Your new teeth are designed to look natural and to fit your face.

We review your bone, gums and goals at your implant consultation, then explain whether full arch dental implants are the right fit for you.

Implant Supported Dentures

Traditional dentures can slip, click or rub, which makes eating and talking stressful. Implant denture treatment solves these problems by securing your denture to dental implants, creating a stable and comfortable fit.

Implant supported dentures also help preserve jawbone, which can shrink after teeth are lost. Many patients find they can enjoy a wider variety of foods and smile without worrying about movement.

We will help you choose between removable and fixed options, explain how each one works and recommend the approach that suits your budget, bone health and lifestyle.

Same Day Dental Implants

Same day dental implants, also called one day smile restoration, allow eligible patients to receive implants and temporary teeth in a single visit. It is a powerful option if you want to avoid long stretches without teeth.

Success depends on strong bone, healthy gums and a carefully designed plan. Not everyone qualifies, and we will tell you honestly whether this approach is right for you or whether a staged treatment would protect your result better.

Zygomatic Dental Implants

Severe bone loss in the upper jaw can make standard implants difficult. Zygomatic dental implants are longer implants anchored in the cheekbone, offering a path to fixed teeth for patients who may have been told they lack enough bone.

This is advanced implant dentistry used for complex dental implant cases. It requires detailed planning and imaging, and we explain every step so you know what to expect.

If you have been turned down for implants elsewhere, an implant consultation can help determine whether zygomatic dental implants are a suitable route to permanent teeth.

Failed Dental Implant Rescue and Revision

An implant that is loose, painful or surrounded by inflamed gums needs prompt attention. Failed dental implant treatment starts with finding the cause, which may be infection, bone loss, bite problems or poor placement.

Depending on your situation, dental implant rescue may involve cleaning and treating the area, regenerating bone or removing and replacing the implant. Dental implant revision can also focus on fixing failed dental implants or correcting bad dental implants that were placed at the wrong angle or depth.

We keep the process clear and respectful, with options explained before any step is taken.

What to Expect at Your Visit

A friendly, unhurried first appointment helps you make confident decisions.
